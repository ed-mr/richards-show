# Adding pictures to the slideshow from a phone

You need two things, which you get from whoever runs the slideshow:

- **the upload link** (a web address ending in `/s/` followed by a code), and
- **the upload password.**

No app is needed. The phone's web browser is enough (Safari on an iPhone,
Chrome on Android).

## Upload

1. Open the upload link on the phone. You can tap it in a message, or scan
   its QR code with the camera.
2. Enter the upload password.
3. Tap **Upload** or the **+** sign, then choose **Photo Library** or
   **Gallery**. Pick one or several pictures and confirm.
4. Wait until each picture shows as uploaded before you close the page.

The pictures appear on the slideshow within about an hour.

**Tip:** to open the upload page with one tap next time, add it to the home
screen. On an iPhone, use Share → *Add to Home Screen*. On Android, use the
menu ⋮ → *Add to Home screen*.

## Good to know

- **You can only add pictures.** You cannot see, change or delete the
  pictures that are already there, not even your own. If you want a picture
  removed, ask whoever runs the slideshow.
- **Use photos (JPEG).** Phones normally send photos as JPEG when you upload
  them through the browser. The slideshow ignores other files, such as
  videos, screenshots in PNG format or iPhone HEIC files.
- **Landscape pictures fill the screen best.** The screen is small and wide
  (3:2). Portrait pictures are shown with borders on the sides.
- If a picture with the same name is already there, Nextcloud keeps both and
  adds a number to the new name.
